### 💫 About Me

Hey there! I'm **Pratyush K Nayak**, a software engineer and builder based in India. I love crafting high-performance mobile tools, system-wide AI utilities, browser extensions, and modern web applications with polished, intuitive user interfaces.

- ✨ **Latest Flagship Release:** Creator of **[Hoverpen](https://github.com/pratyushkk/hoverpen)** — a system-wide Android AI writing assistant featuring floating morphing pill overlays, Google Gemini AI tone rewrites, and multi-engine failover (**[v2.0 Signed Release](https://github.com/pratyushkk/hoverpen/releases/tag/v2.0)**).
- ⚡ **Universal Productivity:** Creator of **[Pastiq](https://github.com/pratyushkk/pastiq)** — a local-first clipboard manager, snippets tool, and browser command center live on the **[Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fnlfffhpiaoiljenmjepgnjdpjjngoed)** and **[Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/pastiq-%E2%80%94-clipboard-comm/obihcbagfbnngcpdahdcihdpgjglkilc)**.
- 🎬 **Graphics & Media:** Creator of **[UltraWide Video Fill Pro](https://github.com/pratyushkk/ultrawide-video-fill-pro)** — a GPU-accelerated extension for 21:9 & 32:9 displays, live on the **[Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/ultrawide-video-fill-pro/kllbjjefnjodgoaglaghcakajfbjmpnm)**.
- 📱 **Mobile & Systems:** Maintainer of **[NetSwiss](https://github.com/pratyushkk/Netswiss-app)**, an Android network diagnostic toolkit with Mock GPS, real-time speed monitoring, and a per-app firewall via `VpnService`.
- ✍️ **Writing:** Sharing insights, tutorials, and engineering deep-dives on **[Hashnode](https://pratyushkk.hashnode.dev/)**.
- ⚡ **Passions:** Android system overlays & Accessibility, Gemini AI workflows, WebGPU / WebGL2 shader pipelines, Manifest V3 architecture, and sleek design systems.
